# NOVA: company background

NOVA is a Bengaluru consumer subscription app for learning skills in short daily lessons ("Skills in 10 minutes a day"), with about 2.3 million users.

- **Platforms**: Android since launch; iOS app launched on 17 June 2024. In January 2026 iOS was 45% of new users.
- **Plans**: ₹299 Standard (monthly), ₹499 Plus (monthly, added August 2024), ₹999 Annual.
- **Onboarding**: 5 steps until February 2025, when 3-step onboarding became the default on Android and iOS.
- **Key metrics**: activation rate (onboarding), 30-day retention, paid conversion, lessons started.
